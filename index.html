Pool Information

Pool average temperature: 81.5° (Range 80°–82°, 79° for swim meets)

Lap Swim

Lap lanes will be open during all pool hours, although the number of lanes available will vary depending on other programs in progress, (i.e., during all swim lesson programs the shallow lanes will be closed to anyone other than those enrolled in the lessons). The pool will have limited lanes from 4–6 p.m., Monday–Friday due to swim team practices and other activities.

Circle swimming is mandatory during crowded times.

Shallow Lane

There is only ONE shallow lane at the Koret Center pool. This lane is often blocked for swim lessons, trainings, or programs. Please take note if you plan to use this lane.

Lane Schedule

Live

Every lane, hour by hour. Move between days with the arrows, and tap a programme in the filter to see only that. On a phone the same schedule opens as a list.

What this replaces

  • The page currently says “it is recommended to check our Pool Lane Schedule before each visit to avoid lane closures and events” — and that link opens a Google Sheet. A swimmer deciding whether to come now has to open a spreadsheet and find today's column.
  • The prose above says lanes are limited from 4–6 p.m. The calendar shows exactly which lanes, on which day, without anyone having to keep the sentence and the spreadsheet in step.
  • Slow, Medium and Fast move through the day — slow in lanes 2–5 in the morning, fast in 12–15 and the deep end. That is the thing a swimmer actually wants and the hardest thing to write down in prose.
  • The shallow lane, the deep end and the 3:30 cleaning slot are bookings like any other, so they appear without a separate note.
